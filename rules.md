# Rules Summary

This is a simplified overview of key server rules.

It does **not** replace the full rules.

👉 **Full Official Rules:**  
https://github.com/RiseGaming/Takistan-Life-Rules

All players are responsible for knowing the full rule set.

---

## Core Conduct Rules

- No cheating, exploiting, or glitch abuse.
- No harassment or discriminatory behaviour.
- No combat logging.
- No random killing without valid roleplay context.
- Do not use safe zones to escape active combat.

If you are unsure whether something is allowed — ask before doing it.

---

## New Life Rule (NLR)

When you die:

- You lose memory of events leading to your death.
- Do not return to your death location for the specified cooldown period.
- Do not immediately attempt to recover lost gear.

Treat death as a reset of knowledge.

---

## Safe Zones & No-Kill Zones

- Safe zones provide combat protection.
- You may not initiate violence inside them.
- You may not flee into safe zones to escape active combat.

Using safe zones to gain advantage will result in enforcement action.

---

## Faction Conflict Overview

### BLUFOR
- Enforces law in the North.
- Must follow escalation and arrest procedures.

### OPFOR
- Controls the South.
- During peacetime, non-OSF members may not enter the North without BLUFOR authorisation.
- Commander may declare war to enable cross-border operations.

### Insurgents
- May engage BLUFOR and OPFOR.
- May not kill civilians unless attacked.
- Expected to protect Muslim civilians where possible.

### Civilians
- Cannot engage in terrorism.
- Must follow criminal roleplay rules.
- May be arrested by law enforcement.

### ESU
- Must remain neutral.
- Cannot carry weapons.
- Must wait after combat ends before reviving.

### PMC
- Operates by contract.
- Must follow standard roleplay and conflict rules.

---

## Border Rules

- North and South are separate jurisdictions.
- Crossing without proper conditions (war, joint operations, permission) is illegal.
- Drone restrictions apply per faction.

Border violations often escalate quickly.

---

## Combat Expectations

- No RDM (Random Deathmatch).
- No VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch).
- Provide valid roleplay before lethal force unless under immediate lethal threat.
- Do not spawn camp.

Conflict should be structured and justified.

---

## Robberies & High-Risk Crime

- Bank robberies and other major crimes generate faction response.
- Expect escalation and consequences.
- Do not exploit mechanics during robberies.

---

## War & Martial Law

- Only OPFOR Commander may declare war.
- War removes certain border restrictions.
- Martial law may be declared under specific conditions.
- War vehicles have special restrictions outside war/martial law.

These events are structured and staff-overseen.

---

## When in Doubt

If you are unsure:

- Re-read the full rules.
- Ask staff in Discord.
- Do not guess.

Ignorance of the rules is not a defence.

---

## Final Reminder

The rules exist to:

- Maintain fair gameplay
- Protect roleplay integrity
- Prevent griefing
- Keep Takistan balanced

Read the full document before engaging in high-risk activity.
