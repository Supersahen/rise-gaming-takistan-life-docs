# Getting Started

New to Takistan Life? Start here.

You don’t need to understand every system on day one. You need a plan.

---

## First Spawn Checklist

1. Open your interaction menu (default: Windows key).
2. Locate an ATM and check your bank balance.
3. Keep most of your money in the bank.
4. Choose a faction that matches how you want to play.

Your first hour sets your momentum.

---

## Cash vs Bank

- Banked money is safe on death.
- Cash carried on your character is not.
- Before doing anything risky, deposit excess cash.

Financial discipline prevents frustration.

---

## How Interactions Work

Most actions in Takistan Life are done through the **interaction key**:

- Ticketing
- Restraining
- Searching
- Vehicle interactions
- Many faction-specific tools

If you cannot find an option, you are likely not in the correct state (for example, the other player must be compliant or restrained).

---

## Choosing a Faction

Each faction plays very differently:

- **Civilian** – Economy, crime, gangs, robberies.
- **BLUFOR** – Law enforcement and northern defense.
- **OPFOR** – Southern military authority and mining economy.
- **Insurgent** – Guerrilla warfare and destabilisation.
- **ESU** – Medical response and revival.
- **PMC** – Contract-based private operations.

If unsure, start as a Civilian.

---

## Safe First Income Options

For new players:

- Legal mining
- Farming crops
- Basic delivery jobs
- Territory patrol (if BLUFOR/OPFOR)

Avoid high-risk crime until you understand the map.

---

## Risk Awareness

Before attempting:

- Bank robberies
- Drug runs
- Convoy interceptions
- Bombing missions

Make sure you have:

- Backup
- Extraction plan
- Bank insurance
- Enough funds to re-gear if you die

Takistan punishes unplanned aggression.

---

## Safe Zones

Safe zones prevent combat.
You may not use safe zones to escape active fights.
Understand where they are before engaging in illegal activity.

---

## General Advice

- Move with purpose.
- Avoid standing still in open areas.
- Upgrade transport before scaling operations.
- Communicate clearly in roleplay situations.
- Ask in Discord if unsure about rules.

Takistan Life works best when players create structured conflict — not random chaos.
