# Civilian

**Role:** Economic backbone, independent operators, and opportunists.

Civilians have the most freedom on the server. You can operate legally, illegally, or somewhere in between. Your success depends on planning, logistics, and risk management.

---

## Getting Started

1. Secure a basic vehicle and harvesting tools.
2. Keep your money in the bank.
3. Start with a legal income loop before scaling into high-risk operations.

Civilians progress through investment, not rank.

---

## Legal Income Paths

### Mining & Refining
- Gather ore at mining locations.
- Process at a refinery for higher returns.
- Larger vehicles increase efficiency.
- Storage upgrades significantly improve profit.

Mining is steady and scalable.

---

### Farming & Legal Crops
- Harvest at marked farm areas.
- Sell at designated markets.
- Lower profit than illegal operations, but safe and stable.

---

## Illegal Income Paths

Illegal work pays significantly more — and attracts attention.

### Drug Production

Each raw material produces a specific drug:

- **Coca → Cocaine**
- **Ergot → LSD**
- **Poppy → Heroin**

Process raw materials at authorised processing locations after purchasing the required training.

Drugs are sold to designated in-game dealers only.

High profit. High police attention.

---

### Meth Production (Separate System)

Meth is different from crop-based drugs.

- Requires precursor chemicals purchased from pharmacies.
- Processing occurs at the designated meth lab.
- Processing carries an explosion risk.
- Expect strong law enforcement response if detected.

Meth pays extremely well — and carries extremely high risk.

---

### Robberies & Direct Crime

Civilians may engage in:

- Bank robberies
- Casino robberies
- Gas station robberies
- Farmers market robberies
- Pharmacy robberies
- Robbing or kidnapping other players

All robberies generate immediate law enforcement response.  
Plan entry, security, and escape routes carefully.

---

### Organ Harvesting

- Requires specific tools.
- Highly illegal.
- Generates severe law enforcement response.

---

## Gangs

Civilians may create or join gangs.

- Gang areas provide strategic advantage.
- Organised groups survive longer than solo operators.
- Territory disputes create conflict and opportunity.

---

## Vehicle Strategy

- Upgrade storage early.
- Use larger transports for bulk operations.
- Consider plate removal and lock changes for illegal runs.
- Logistics determines success.

---

## Chop Shops

Chop shops allow sale of unowned vehicles at reduced value.

Illegal activity. Expect retaliation if caught.

---

## Factories

Factories allow long-term investment and reduced production costs.

Best suited for established players with capital.

---

## Bank & Insurance

- Banked money is safe on death.
- Cash carried is not.
- During bank robberies, bank balances may be affected.
- Insurance protects your balance.

Protect your capital before scaling operations.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Carrying large cash amounts.
- Running high-value goods without backup.
- Using small vehicles for bulk operations.
- Attempting robberies without planning extraction.

---

## Recommended Progression

1. Establish legal income.
2. Upgrade transport capacity.
3. Build financial buffer.
4. Move into controlled illegal operations.
5. Scale with gang coordination or hired protection.

Civilians succeed through preparation — not impulse.
