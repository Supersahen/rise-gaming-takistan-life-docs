# ESU (Emergency Services Unit)

**Role:** Neutral medical response and revival.

ESU keeps the server functioning. Without medical response, every firefight becomes a respawn loop. ESU is strictly neutral and does not take sides in faction conflicts.

---

## Core Responsibilities

- Respond to downed players.
- Conduct revival procedures.
- Provide medical transport.
- Coordinate with law enforcement for scene access.

ESU is not a combat unit.

---

## Neutrality & Rules

- ESU must remain faction neutral.
- ESU may not carry weapons.
- ESU may not commit crimes.
- ESU must wait at least one minute after combat ends before reviving.
- If organs have been harvested, revival is not possible.

Scene safety comes first.

---

## Revival Process (Overview)

1. Confirm the scene is safe.
2. Begin the revival procedure.
3. Successful revives generate income and rank progression.

Do not rush into active gunfire. Request protection if required.

---

## First Shift Priorities

1. Learn hospital and vehicle spawn locations.
2. Carry medical equipment and repair kits.
3. Use ground vehicles first; aircraft only when properly licensed.
4. Coordinate with BLUFOR or OPFOR when entering hostile zones.

Professionalism builds trust across factions.

---

## Aircraft & Rapid Response

- Air vehicle training is required to operate ESU aircraft.
- Aircraft are best used for distant or high-priority incidents.
- Confirm landing safety before approach.

Speed is useful. Safety is mandatory.

---

## Banking & Insurance

- Banked money is safe on death.
- Cash carried is not.
- During bank robberies, balances may be affected.
- Bank insurance protects your earnings.
- Insurance can be stored privately or carried.

Medical work can be high-risk during major incidents.

---

## Storage & Equipment

- Private storage is available at ESU HQ.
- Only virtual items can be stored (not firearms).
- Maintain adequate repair kits, medikits, and fuel supplies.

Preparation reduces response time.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Entering active firefights without clearance.
- Forgetting neutrality obligations.
- Attempting revival without confirming organ status.
- Failing to coordinate with law enforcement.

---

## Operational Mindset

Effective ESU members:

- Communicate clearly.
- Remain calm under pressure.
- Avoid escalation.
- Treat all factions equally.

Your presence stabilises the server.
