# BLUFOR (Police – Northern Republic)

**Role:** Law enforcement, territorial control, and republic security.

BLUFOR officers enforce the law in the Northern Republic, protect civilians, and respond to insurgent and OPFOR threats. You are expected to act within the law and maintain professional conduct at all times.

---

## Core Responsibilities

BLUFOR duties include:

- Traffic enforcement and ticketing
- Criminal investigations and arrests
- Prisoner processing
- Border security and checkpoint operations
- Responding to robberies and violent incidents
- Territory capture and defense
- Coordinating with ESU during emergencies

Territory control is a significant part of BLUFOR income. Capture operations, patrol missions, and defense missions often overlap geographically. A patrol into an insurgent-held area may naturally become a territory reclaim operation.

Operational awareness and coordination are expected.

---

## First Shift Priorities

New officers should:

1. Equip a **non-lethal option** (mandatory).
2. Carry restraints and basic patrol tools.
3. Patrol with another officer if possible.
4. Start with traffic enforcement and call response before high-risk raids.

Confidence comes from understanding procedure, not escalation.

---

## Non-Lethal Options (Required)

BLUFOR officers must carry a stun option. Two primary choices:

- **Taser** – Shorter range, functions as a sidearm. Useful for close engagements and traffic stops.
- **Beanbag Shotgun** – Longer effective range, ideal for controlling suspects at distance.

Choose based on your assignment, but always ensure you have a viable non-lethal option available.

---

## Interaction System (Important)

Most enforcement actions — including ticketing, restraining, searching, and prisoner transport — are performed by interacting directly with the player using the interaction key.

If you are unsure how to perform an action, consult a senior officer before improvising.

---

## Use of Force

- Non-lethal force should be used when practical.
- Lethal force is justified when facing a clear and immediate lethal threat.
- Communicate clearly before escalating when possible.

Professional conduct prevents unnecessary escalation.

---

## Arrest Flow (Operational Overview)

1. Restrain suspect.
2. Transport to the northern prison facility.
3. Assign appropriate charges.
4. Jail time or bail is processed.
5. Return to duty.

Take time to ensure charges are correct. Accuracy matters.

---

## Ticketing & Minor Offences

Not every situation requires arrest.

Officers may:
- Issue tickets for traffic violations.
- Fine minor criminal offences.
- Conduct checkpoint inspections.
- Monitor high-traffic areas using radar equipment.

Effective policing reduces escalation before it begins.

---

## Border & Checkpoints

BLUFOR may:

- Operate border checkpoints.
- Inspect vehicles and individuals.
- Guard northern access points.

Checkpoint operations provide additional pay and rank progression. Proper structure and signage should be maintained.

---

## Territory Operations

Territory control is one of BLUFOR’s primary revenue sources.

- Patrol and defense missions often align with capture zones.
- Reclaiming insurgent-held territory improves republic stability and officer income.
- Coordinate before entering hostile zones.
- Avoid overextending without support.

Territory operations should be structured, not chaotic.

---

## Equipment & Tools

BLUFOR has access to specialised equipment, including:

- Spike strips
- Radar gun
- Roadblocks and barriers
- Vehicle tracking systems
- Bait car systems
- Bomb defusal tools
- Standard patrol and air vehicles

War-designated vehicles are restricted to declared war or martial law conditions.

---

## Drones & Airspace

Police drones may **only operate in northern airspace**.

Entering southern airspace with police drones is not permitted.

Armed aerial systems are restricted to war or martial law conditions.

---

## Bank & Insurance

- Money stored in your bank is not lost on death.
- Cash carried on your person is lost on death.
- During a **bank robbery**, a portion of bank balances may be affected.
- Bank insurance protects against this.
- Insurance may be carried on your person or stored in private storage at Police HQ.

Officers should manage their finances responsibly.

---

## Vehicle Conduct

- Store vehicles properly when not in use.
- Do not abandon police vehicles in public areas.
- Use appropriate vehicles for high-risk arrests.
- Return war-designated vehicles immediately when war or martial law ends.

Vehicle discipline reflects operational discipline.

---

## Rank & Progression

Rank increases through:

- Active patrol work
- Arrests and ticketing
- Territory captures
- Border checkpoint operations
- Authorised missions

Higher rank unlocks additional equipment and responsibility.

Promotion reflects trust and performance.

---

## Special Response Team (SRT)

SRT is a whitelisted & specialist unit focused on high-risk incidents.

- Handles terrorism, bomb threats, and major escalations.
- Requires application and approval.
- Operates with structured coordination.
- Subject to the same vehicle restrictions as standard police.

SRT is a responsibility, not simply access to stronger equipment.

---

## Professional Expectations

BLUFOR functions best when:

- Civilians feel protected, not harassed.
- Criminals feel pressure, not griefing.
- Insurgents face organised resistance.
- Arrests are justified and explained.

Maintain discipline, communicate clearly, and act within your authority.
