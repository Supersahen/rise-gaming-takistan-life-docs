# PMC (Private Military Contractors)

**Role:** Independent contract operators for hire.

PMC operates outside faction alignment. You work for whoever hires you — BLUFOR, OPFOR, Civilians, or others — within server rules.

PMC is professional, structured, and reputation-driven.

---

## Entry Requirements

PMC is typically a whitelisted role.

- Application and approval required.
- Significant financial investment required for licensing and equipment.

PMC is not an early-game faction.

---

## Core Activities

- Security contracts
- Escort missions
- Convoy protection
- Targeted operations
- Bounty work (when not under contract)

PMC operates on agreement and payment.

---

## How Contracts Work (In Practice)

- Define objective clearly.
- Agree on payment terms.
- Clarify duration and success conditions.
- Coordinate extraction and logistics.

Ambiguity causes disputes. Be precise.

---

## First Deployment Priorities

1. Secure required licenses.
2. Establish a reliable team.
3. Begin with smaller contracts.
4. Build a reputation before scaling up.

PMC reputation determines future work.

---

## Equipment & Assets

PMC has access to:

- Advanced weapons and equipment
- Armoured vehicles
- Aircraft (if properly licensed)

Equipment is expensive and lost on death. Deploy carefully.

---

## Banking & Insurance

- Banked money is safe on death.
- Cash carried is not.
- During bank robberies, balances may be affected.
- Bank insurance protects your capital.

PMC operations often involve high financial exposure.

---

## Operational Discipline

- Avoid unnecessary escalation.
- Do not interfere in conflicts without contract.
- Maintain neutrality unless contracted.
- Operate within server rules at all times.

PMC is not freelance chaos.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Taking contracts beyond your team’s capacity.
- Ignoring extraction planning.
- Entering faction conflicts without payment agreement.
- Overinvesting before securing stable income.

---

## Professional Expectation

PMC succeeds when:

- Contracts are clear.
- Teams are disciplined.
- Reputation is maintained.
- Operations are structured.

PMC is paid professionalism — not mercenary randomness.
