# Frequently Asked Questions

This page answers common player questions.

If unsure about something not listed here, consult the full rules or ask in Discord.

---

## Why did I lose my cash when I died?

Cash carried on your character is lost on death.

Money stored in your bank is protected.

Deposit excess cash before engaging in risky activity.

---

## Why was my bank balance reduced?

During a bank robbery, balances may be affected.

Bank insurance prevents robbery loss.

Insurance can be carried or stored privately.

---

## Why can’t I cross the border?

Border rules apply during peacetime.

Non-OSF OPFOR may not enter the North without BLUFOR authorisation.

War status changes border permissions.

Confirm current war status before crossing.

---

## Why can’t I revive someone?

Possible reasons:

- Combat has not ended (ESU must wait).
- Organs were harvested.
- You are not ESU.
- You are in an unsafe zone.

Confirm conditions before attempting revival.

---

## Why can’t I process drugs?

Check:

- You purchased the required processing training.
- You are at the correct processing location.
- Your faction is allowed to process that drug.

Insurgents process drugs at base.
Civilians process at designated labs.

---

## Why did my drone not deploy?

Possible causes:

- Airspace restriction.
- War status limitation.
- Equipment lost on death.
- Not in a permitted zone.

Drone usage is jurisdiction-based.

---

## Why did I get arrested for crossing into another territory?

Each faction controls its own jurisdiction during peacetime.

Crossing without proper conditions may be treated as hostile activity.

---

## Why didn’t my robbery trigger properly?

Common causes:

- Incorrect location.
- Not meeting robbery conditions.
- Conflict with active safe zone.
- Event cooldown.

Ensure all robbery requirements are met before initiating.

---

## Why can’t I use certain vehicles?

Some vehicles are restricted to:

- War status
- Martial law
- Specific ranks
- Specific factions

Check your faction guide.

---

## I died — can I return to the fight?

The New Life Rule applies.

You may not immediately return to your death location.

You lose knowledge of the events leading to your death.

Review the full rules for details.
