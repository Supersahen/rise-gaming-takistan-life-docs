# ESU Guide - Rise Gaming Takistan Life

## Overview
The Emergency Services Unit (ESU) handles medical response and revival across the map. ESU players are the only faction able to revive other players and earn money and rank points for successful revives.

## Core Activities

### Revival Process
1. Locate an incapacitated player and assess the scene for safety.
2. Begin the revival procedure using ESU medical equipment (30-second standard procedure).
3. Base success rate is approximately 80%; unsuccessful attempts do not generate rewards.
4. Reward for a successful revival: monetary payment and rank points.
5. Players cannot be revived if organs have been harvested from their body.

### Medical & Emergency Response
- Respond to pinned dispatches and high-traffic combat zones.
- Repair/assist in vehicle crashes and extract players from danger.
- Manage hospital transport and triage.

## Licences and Equipment
- ESU Training (purchase the ESU training licence) is required for standard medical duties.
- ESU Air Vehicle Training is required to operate ESU aircraft.
- Medical kits, revival equipment and ESU vehicles are available from ESU supply outlets.

## Banking and Storage

### Shared Banking with Civilians
- ESU uses the same bank system as civilians (not a separate faction bank).
- Bank money is safe and never lost on death; only cash in your inventory is lost on death.
- During bank raids, a small percentage of your bank balance may be stolen unless you have bank insurance.
- Exception: VIP members protect their bank automatically.

### Private Storage
- Private storage is available at ESU HQ for virtual inventory items (repair kits, refuel kits, medikits, bank insurance, etc.).
- Guns and ammunition (Arma 3 items) cannot be stored.

## General Equipment Notes
- Gas stations provide essential items: medikits (for full healing), repair kits (prevent vehicle stranding), steroids (temporarily expand inventory), earplugs (tear gas protection), vehicle ammo, and other utility items.

## Operational notes and practical guidance
- Neutrality: ESU must remain neutral and impartial. ESU members should not take sides in faction conflicts and may only provide medical assistance in active combat zones if all parties permit it.
- Organ harvesting: If organs have been removed, revival is not possible — verify the patient’s condition before initiating procedures.
- Scene safety: Hospitals and bases are safezones; however, personnel responding to incidents may be at risk while approaching an active scene.
- Timing: Revivals are time-sensitive; prompt response increases the chance of success.

## Where to go
- **Hospital / ESU HQ**: primary base for ESU vehicles and gear.
- **Ambulance/Heli pads**: use aircraft to reach distant incidents quickly.

## Vehicles and equipment use
- Inventory use: All items are used via the inventory—open your inventory, select the item, and choose use to apply it.
- Aircraft: Use ESU helicopters for rapid response when authorised and properly licensed (ESU Air Vehicle Training).
- Coordination: Coordinate with law enforcement for safe access to active scenes; ESU is non-combat and requires protection in hostile environments.

## Common mistakes
- Entering contested or active firefight areas without clearance or escort. ESU personnel should prioritise safety and request protection when necessary.
- Failing to confirm whether organs have been harvested before attempting revival procedures.

## Quick Checklist (on duty)

1. Confirm the incident location and evaluate scene safety.
2. Ensure bank insurance is active to protect your earnings on death.
3. Choose the appropriate vehicle (aircraft when needed and when licensed).
4. Bring medical kits and revival equipment; use items from your inventory via the inventory menu.
5. Coordinate extraction and request an escort if the scene is hostile.
