# OPFOR/TLA Guide - Rise Gaming Takistan Life

## Overview
As OPFOR/Takistani Liberation Army, you control South territories and can declare war. Military dictatorship gameplay with a separate prison system. The Commander can enable cross-border attacks. OPFOR cannot harvest or process drugs.

## Getting Started
- You start with $500,000 to equip yourself and get started.
- Report to OPFOR base to access vehicles and faction shops.
- Talk to your Commander for war timers and strategic objectives.

## Military Operations

### Territory Control
- Capture South territories (15 zones).
- 30-second capture time; 1 rank point per capture.
- Coordinate with squads to defend and rotate control.

### War Declaration
- **Commander-only**: Declare War to allow cross-border attacks into North.
- Plan logistics, prisoner handling, and extraction routes before declaring.

### Law Enforcement
- Arrests use the same mechanics as Police.
- Transport prisoners to the southern jail near the airfield to process charges.
- **Taxation Alternative**: OPFOR can tax civilians up to $150,000 instead of arresting them. Taxes are automatically paid to the faction.

## Equipment & Vehicles

### Equipment
- **Military Vehicles**: Tanks, helicopters, offroad vehicles, heavy transport (Ural/MTVR-style), APCs.
- **Weapons**: Military-grade rifles, machine guns, explosives.
- **Attachments**: Scopes, silencers, tactical equipment.
- **Special Gear**: Combat armor, helmets, night-vision equipment.

### Garage Storage and Vehicle Management
- All OPFOR vehicles are stored in the faction garage.
- Garage fees: 15% of vehicle purchase price.
- Impounded vehicles: Additional $10,000 fee on top of garage fees.
- Vehicles become impounded during server events; retrieve them from impound lots.

### Bank Insurance
- Bank money is safe and never lost on death; only cash in your inventory is lost on death.
- During bank raids, a small percentage of your bank balance may be stolen unless you have bank insurance.
- Exception: VIP members protect their bank automatically.

### Drones and Aerial Systems
- UAV Terminals: OPFOR can purchase terminals to control reconnaissance and bomb-dropping drones.
- **Flight capability**: OPFOR drones can be sent north into BLUFOR territory; however this is provocation and may lead to BLUFOR retaliation.

### Private Storage
- Private storage is available at OPFOR base for virtual inventory items (equipment, repair kits, refuel kits, medikits, lockpicks, ores, spike strips, etc.).
- Guns and ammunition (Arma 3 items) cannot be stored.

## Restrictions
- Cannot harvest or process illegal drugs.
- Cannot join civilian gang structures.
- During peacetime, non-OSF personnel are limited to the South without BLUFOR permission. Unauthorized North operations result in arrest.
- OSF (Special Forces) members may conduct border incursions without permission and are kill-on-sight to BLUFOR.
- OPFOR starts with 13 zones; 2 zones are given to Insurgents at each server restart.

## Gotchas & Practical Tips

-- Peacetime limits: Do not operate inside North territory without an authorised war declaration; solo incursions are likely to face heavy opposition.
-- Prisons and transfers: Use the designated OPFOR prison facility to process detainees and plan secure transfer routes to avoid ambushes.
-- Pay and taxes: The Commander influences local tax rates and leadership decisions which can affect faction pay distributions.

### Vehicle and modification notes

- Heavy transports are preferred for prisoner movement and supply convoys due to their survivability and capacity.
- Use storage expansion modifications to increase cargo capacity. Plate removal and lock changes are available for operational security when appropriate.

### Common Mistakes

- Overextending into North without a declared war or support leaves units isolated.
- Using light, fast vehicles for high-value convoys invites ambushes — use armoured transports.

## Government Convoys

- OPFOR government runs a government supply convoy to the OPFOR base approximately every 45 minutes.
- Civilians and other factions can attempt to rob this convoy.
- During declared war: BLUFOR (police) can also rob or intercept OPFOR convoys.
- Convoy interdiction: The OSF (special forces) can intercept and engage convoys in northern territory with appropriate roleplay justification.
- **BLUFOR Response**: BLUFOR can arrest OSF members engaging in convoy operations north of central territory.

### OSF (OPFOR Special Forces)

- **Status**: Elite whitelisted unit within OPFOR (equivalent to BLUFOR's SRT).
- **Application**: Apply via the dedicated applications channel on Discord; only experienced OPFOR members are considered.
- **Benefits**: Higher faction regard, dedicated secure operations area, access to expanded equipment tier, and slight discount on faction purchases.
- **Operations**: Execute high-risk raids, special warfare missions, convoy escorts, and classified faction objectives.
- **Authority**: OSF operates under field command authority; during active operations, mission commander decisions supersede rank hierarchy.

## Quick Checklist (on duty)

- Monitor Commander announcements for war status, tax rates, and faction objectives.
- Prepare combat vehicle and maintain weaponry.
- Ensure bank insurance is active to protect faction earnings on death.
- Coordinate with squad for patrol or convoy operations.
- Track ongoing conflicts and BLUFOR activity.
- Always have a convoy escort for prisoner transfers and high-value haulage.
- Keep spare repair kits and fuel in supply vehicles.
