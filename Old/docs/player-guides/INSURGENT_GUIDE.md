# Insurgent Guide - Rise Gaming Takistan Life

## Overview
Insurgents operate as an independent force focused on guerrilla warfare, bombing missions, and smuggling. They can access most civilian economic loops and all drug processing, but they cannot create or join civilian gangs. Expect high risk and a heavy response from Police/OPFOR.

## Getting Started
- Spawn with faction equipment at insurgent bases.
- Use the insurgent dealer to buy processing licenses and weaponry.
- Consider `jihadi_training` ($300,000) for additional convenience and vehicle access.

## Economic and Unlawful Activities

### Drug Harvesting and Processing
- Insurgents may harvest and process all controlled substances through their faction's dealer. Insurgents do not use civilian gang labs.
- Processing training and licences are purchased from the insurgent base dealer when required.
- Sales: Processed product is sold through authorised in-game dealers only; arrange secure transport and protection for sales.

### Meth and High-Risk Processing
- Precursors are obtained from local pharmacies in major towns.
- Processing is conducted at a dedicated meth laboratory in the far north-west of the map (near Nur and Nagara).
- **Warning**: Meth processing may explode when processing at the lab. Due to the high risk, conduct operations only in well-secured locations with backup and extraction plans.
- Recommended: Process during low-population hours and arrange immediate transport away from the lab.

### Organ Harvesting
- Insurgents may harvest organs using a knife purchased from general stores. Organs are sold to specialised buyers in specific towns (for example a buyer near the PMC area). This activity is illegal and carries severe penalties.

## Combat & Special Ops

### Bombing Missions
- Bombing missions can be requested at your faction's main base ATM only.
- The player who requests the mission receives the explosive device and is responsible for planting and defending it.
- If the requesting player is killed or the mission conditions are not met, the mission fails.
- These operations attract significant response; plan personnel, routes, and extraction carefully.

### Garage Storage and Vehicle Management
- Insurgent vehicles are stored at the faction base garage.
- Garage fees: 10% of the vehicle's purchase price (same as civilians).
- Impounded vehicles: An additional $10,000 flat fee applies on top of the normal garage fee.
- Impound locations: Insurgent vehicles are recovered from regional impound facilities (contact faction for details).
- Tow truck service: Civilian tow truck drivers collect and deliver impounded vehicles.

### Bank Insurance
- Bank money is safe and never lost on death; only cash in your inventory is lost on death.
- During bank raids, a small percentage of your bank balance may be stolen unless you have bank insurance.
- Exception: VIP members protect their bank automatically.

### Private Storage
- Private storage is available at the insurgent base for virtual inventory items (repair kits, refuel kits, medikits, lockpicks, ores, drugs, etc.).
- Guns and ammunition (Arma 3 items) cannot be stored.

### Supply Convoys
- Insurgents receive periodic supply convoys to the insurgent base (approximately every 45 minutes).
- These convoys carry restricted weapons and supplies to help maintain faction operations.
- Convoys attract police/OPFOR attention; defend them or expect them to be intercepted.
- Successful defense grants bonus supply and morale to the faction.

### Drones and Aerial Systems
- Insurgents can purchase UAV terminals to control bomb-dropping and reconnaissance drones.
- Bomb-dropping drones: Deliver explosives to targets.
- Reconnaissance drones: Gather intelligence on police/OPFOR movements.
- **Risk**: UAV terminals are expensive and lost on death; exercise extreme caution when operating them.

### Vehicle Chop Shops
- Insurgents can sell unowned vehicles at chop shops for reduced payout (illegal activity).
- Chop shops are located at Nur (north) and in the Falar/Anar area (south).
- Payment is immediate but significantly lower than legitimate vehicle sales.

## Restrictions & Gotchas
- **No Gangs**: Insurgents cannot create or join civilian gangs — they use their own faction networks.
- **High Wanted**: Drug processing and bombing draw immediate police/OPFOR response.
- **Safezones**: Do not attempt operations near faction safezones; security is enforced automatically.

## Where to go
- **Insurgent Base**: Primary location to purchase processing training, equipment, and smuggler services.
- **Processing and sale**: Use the insurgent dealer and authorised dealers in towns for selling processed products.
- **Pharmacies**: Precursors for meth are available from pharmacies in major towns.

## Tools & Licenses Checklist

- `jihadi_training` — allows buying civilian vehicles and extra convenience.
- Processing licenses — buy at insurgent dealer for each drug type.
- Explosive/Bomb training items — required for certain missions.

## Vehicle & Mod Shop Tips
- Prefer unmarked or lightly-armoured transports to blend in during smuggling runs.
- Use `Plate Removal` if you need to obscure identity during operations.
- For hauling processed product, `Storage Expansion` and heavy trucks are recommended.

## Common Mistakes
- Processing meth near heavy population centers — high casualty/explosion risk and easy detection.
- Assuming gang mechanics apply — insurgents have no gang menu; coordinate via faction chat.

## Quick Checklist (typical insurgent operation)

- Buy required processing licenses at insurgent dealer.
- Acquire precursor materials/pharmacy items if making meth.
- Choose a secure, remote processing location; ensure extraction is planned.
- Arrange protection for post-process sale and supply convoy defense.
- Monitor faction radio for bombing mission requests and supply convoy schedules.
- Ensure bank insurance is active to protect faction earnings on death.
- Verify personal and vehicle storage is organized before high-risk operations.
