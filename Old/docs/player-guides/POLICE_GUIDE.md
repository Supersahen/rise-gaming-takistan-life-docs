# Police/NATO Guide - Rise Gaming Takistan Life

## Overview
As a member of the police force, your role is to enforce the law within your jurisdiction, protect civilians, and respond to incidents. Police personnel have access to law enforcement equipment, vehicles, and a rank system that unlocks additional abilities and responsibilities. Police cannot commit crimes or use any illegal equipment.

## Getting Started
- You start with $500,000 to equip yourself and get started.
- Police HQ has access to vehicles and all the starting shops.
- Progress through the rank structure by completing patrols, arrests, and territory captures to unlock additional privileges.
- A basic loadout should include a stun weapon and a gas mask at the minimum. Police are required to carry a stun weapon.
- As rank is increased more weapons and equipment will be available to purchase from faction shops.

## Law Enforcement

### Arrest Process
1. **Restrain Suspect**: Interact on a vulnerable player (stunned, lying down, or with hands up) and select restrain player
2. **Transport**: Transport the prisoner to the northern jail, interacting on a player and selecting put in will put them into the nearest
3. **Assign Charges**: Choose from available crime types
4. **Jail Time**: 2-20 minutes based on severity
5. **Bail Option**: Prisoners can pay bail directly (automatically divided among all online police)

### Equipment
- **Spike Strips**: Deployable barriers that puncture vehicle tires on contact.
- **Radar Gun**: 100m range speed detection tool (200 kmph max detection).
- **Roadblocks**: Deployable barriers for traffic control and containment.
- **Tracking Chip**: Add a persistent map marker to any vehicle for surveillance.
- **Bait Car Kit**: Install on a police vehicle to add a map marker and allow remote locking and immobilisation from anywhere on the map (useful for vehicle seizure operations).
- **Weapon and Item Bags**: Deployable containers that will delete nearby weapons or items when used.
- **Defuser**: Disarm explosives and bombs within area proximity when activated.
- **Vehicles**: Police cars, SUVs, helicopters, and armoured transports.
- **Weapons**: Law enforcement firearms appropriate to rank and training.

### Ranks & Progression
| Rank | Requirement | Unlocks |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Officer | Rank ≥ 100 | Basic equipment |
| Sergeant | Rank ≥ 200 | Enhanced tools |
| Lieutenant | Rank ≥ 400 | Advanced access |
| Captain | Rank ≥ 800 | Leadership abilities |
| Colonel | Rank ≥ 1600 | Martial law declaration |

**Leveling**: Earn rank points through patrol missions, arrests, territory captures, and other authorised duties.

## Territory Control
- Control North territories (12 zones)
- 30-second capture time
- 1 rank point per successful capture
- Defend against OPFOR and insurgents

## Restrictions
- Police personnel may not participate in illegal drug processing, harvesting, or civilian gang creation. Officers are expected to uphold and act within the law.

## Garage Storage and Impounding

- All police vehicles are stored at Police HQ.
- Garage fees: 15% of vehicle purchase price.
- Impounded vehicles: Additional $10,000 fee on top of garage fees.
- Impound lots are at the north airfield and regional facilities.
- Impounded vehicles stay at the impound lot until retrieved; tow truck drivers can help transport them.
- Parking violation: Never leave police vehicles unparked outside of garages during quiet periods.

## Bank Insurance

- Bank money is safe and never lost on death; only cash in your inventory is lost on death.
- During bank raids, a small percentage of your bank balance may be stolen unless you have bank insurance.
- Exception: VIP players protect their bank automatically.
- Bank insurance is available at Police HQ.

## Drones and Aerial Systems

- UAV Terminals: Police can purchase UAV terminals to control drones (expensive purchase; police maintain inventory on death making it more feasible).
- Drone capabilities: Military reconnaissance drones are standard; armed drones (Predators) can be operated during war or martial law.
- **Flight restriction**: Police drones cannot enter the south; they are confined to northern airspace.

## Private Storage

- Private storage is available at Police HQ for virtual inventory items (spike strips, equipment, repair kits, refuel kits, medikits, etc.).
- Guns and ammunition (Arma 3 items) cannot be stored.

## Gotchas & Practical Tips

- **Jurisdiction**: Police enforce North territories. Unauthorized operations outside your territory may result in disciplinary action.
- **Bail mechanics**: Police receive bail payments when accepting bail; bail uses a percentage of bank or fixed caps — verify amounts before accepting.

Operational locations

- **Police Headquarters**: Primary location for issuing equipment, vehicles, and briefing patrols.
- **Prison facilities**: Use the designated police prison to process arrested individuals.
- **Checkpoints and radar locations**: Set up speed enforcement and traffic stops in high-traffic routes as assigned.

Tools and training

- Complete required training (patrol and response training) to access improved vehicles and equipment.
- Obtain the appropriate weapon permits from licensing stores if your role requires them (pistol, rifle, or automatic permits).
- Police supply shops stock deployable items such as spike strips, restraints, and barricade materials.

Vehicle and equipment notes

- Use sturdy transport vehicles for extended patrols and operations; larger transports provide better utility.
- War-only vehicles: Officers cannot buy war-designated vehicles outside of active war or martial law. If war/martial law ends, return vehicles to base.

### Common Mistakes

- Overextending without backup during enforcement; always coordinate with other officers.
- Using light, fast vehicles for high-value arrests; use armored transports for safety.

## Quick Checklist (on shift)

- Equip spike strips and radar gun for traffic enforcement.
- Ensure tracking chips and bait car kits are available for vehicle operations.
- Coordinate with dispatch/ESU for medical calls and emergency response.
- Ensure you have enough bank insurance to protect your bank balance during bank robberies.
- Monitor and ensure that blufor maintains control of the northern territories, these increase the amount of money received in paychecks.

## Special Response Team (SRT)

SRT is an elite, whitelisted component of the police force focused on high-risk, military-response tasks. Application for SRT roles is via the server's application channel. SRT personnel operate from a secured area adjacent to the main police base and have access to expanded equipment and supply options.

- Role: Rapid response to terrorism, bomb threats, and other high-risk incidents. SRT units are expected to operate with military discipline and coordination.
- Equipment: SRT members have access to higher-tier equipment and specialised tools, and may purchase from an SRT supply outlet at a reduced cost. SRT uses the same baseline police vehicles and systems; they do not have unrestricted access to war vehicles outside of declared war or martial law.
- Operational note: The SRT area is connected to the police HQ but separated by a secured barrier. Treat SRT roles as specialist positions requiring application and approval.
