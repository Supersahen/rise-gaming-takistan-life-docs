# PMC Guide - Rise Gaming Takistan Life

## Overview
Private Military Contractors (PMC) are elite, often whitelisted, operatives offering contract work, high-risk security, and access to advanced equipment. PMCs require significant upfront investment in licenses but gain access to premium missions and gear.

## Requirements and licences
- PMC roles are typically whitelisted; apply via the server's application channel to be considered for a PMC slot.
- Key PMC licences and approximate costs:
  - PMC Journeyman License — $1,350,000 (basic PMC qualification)
  - PMC Defense License — $350,000 (advanced defence training)
  - PMC Pilot License — $850,000 (aircraft operation)
- Full access commonly requires a multi-million dollar investment across licences and equipment.

## Core Activities
- Contract operations for other factions (escort, protection, assaults).
- Bounty hunting when not on contract.
- Premium payouts and access to private markets.

## Where to go
- **PMC Base**: contract boards, vehicle access, PMC shops.
- **Contract hubs**: look for mission boards or announcements for contract tasks.

## Tools & Gear
- Access to high-tier weapons and specialized vehicles.
- PMC shops sell advanced gear; costs are higher than civilian equivalents.

## Practical considerations
- Contracts often require extensive logistics and disciplined teams — recruit trusted personnel with defined roles (transport, escort, medics).
- Licence and equipment costs are significant; plan a reliable funding path (for example mining and production or high-value missions) before committing.
- PMC equipment is attractive to adversaries; use secure vehicle storage and lock-change services when appropriate.

## Finance and banking
- PMC and ESU operate within the same server-wide banking system used by civilians. Manage funds carefully and use bank services to secure capital.

## Vehicle & Mod Tips
- Use armoured transports for convoys and VIP protection.
- Keep a dedicated aircraft with `pmc_license_air` for rapid response and insertion/extraction.

## Quick Checklist (becoming PMC)
- Save for and buy required PMC licenses.
- Set up a reliable team with roles (transport, escort, medics).
- Bid on contracts and scale up from small security jobs to higher-paying missions.
