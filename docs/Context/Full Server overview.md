## Territory System

### Capture Rules

* **BLUFOR, OPFOR, Insurgents** can capture and neutralise territories from each other.
* **BLUFOR and OPFOR cannot capture each other’s territories unless they are at war.**

### Territory Payouts

* Territory payouts go to **everyone on your faction**, regardless of where you are.
* Payouts apply to **owned territories only**.
* **Neutral territories pay nobody.**
* Each owned flag contributes to the payout: **$20,000 every 15 minutes per flag**.

### Starting Flags

* **Insurgents:** 2 flags *(located in OPFOR territory; OPFOR can retake to reach 14 total)*
* **BLUFOR:** 12 flags
* **OPFOR:** 12 flags

## Beginner Quickstart — Pick a Path

This section gives new players simple, followable checklists for common early goals. Use the in-game interaction menu (Windows Key) at each location to perform actions.

1) Legal Miner (Low risk — steady income)

- Buy a Driver License ($5,000) if you plan to use vehicles.
- Buy a Pickaxe ($2,000) from mining shops near spawn or at mining tool locations.
- Travel to a mining zone (markers: takistan_mine_1, takistan_mine_2).
- Hold the mine action (interaction menu) to harvest ore.
- Buy Mining License ($100,000) at DMV/University to process ore.
- Go to the Resource Refinery (marker: refinery_1) and open the Refinery dialog to process raw ore into ingots.
- Sell refined materials at the market for higher prices.

2) Civilian Drug Runner (High risk — high reward)

- Buy a Driver License ($5,000) to use a vehicle for faster runs.
- Acquire harvesting tools: Sickle/Scythe from farmer markets for plant drugs.
- Travel to the appropriate drug field (crop fields are marked yellow). For gang-based drug fields, consider joining or creating a gang (cost $500,000) to control gang areas.
- Harvest raw drug materials (hold the Harvest action at the field).
- Purchase the relevant processing license at DMV/University or via dealers (marijuana ~ $20k; other drugs cost more — see Licenses list in this document).
- Take raw materials to a drug lab or processing dealer (civilian gang labs or insurgent base dealer) and process them using the processing dialog.
- Sell processed drugs at Black Market locations or to NPC dealers (dealers are commonly located between Rasman and Bastam). Expect law enforcement attention.

3) Meth Production (North-only, very high risk)

- Meth precursor ingredients are illegal to possess. Pharmacies selling precursors are near Rasman (North) and near Anar & the southern hospital (South).
- Ingredients: 1× Iodine, 2× Red Phosphorous, 2× Ammonia Hydroxide, 1× Hydriotic Acid.
- Mix using a mixer (can be done anywhere), then process at a meth lab. Bulk processing increases explosion severity (15% chance to explode on processing events).
- Processed meth yields the highest illegal profit but carries high chance of explosion and severe legal penalties.

4) Buy and Use Vehicles (General)

- Get a Driver License ($5,000) for cars; Truck License ($32,500) for trucks; Pilot License ($500,000) for aircraft.
- Purchase at civilian or faction dealerships (map markers indicate shops). Keys are issued on purchase; press `U` to lock/unlock and `T` to open trunk.
- Use garage storage to persist vehicles ($1,000 storage cost). Retrieve vehicles from garages when needed.

Quick Tips

- Bank money regularly to avoid losing cash on death.
- If you plan to run drugs, use a vehicle with high storage and consider hiring protection or joining a gang.
- Factories provide long-term passive income and manufacturing discounts — consider investing once you have stable capital.

* Illegal shops/areas are **red/orange**.
* Legal places tend to be **blue/green/yellow**.

* **Pharmacies:** orange dot + “Pharmacy”

* Bases have safezones marked by circles.
* No combat in safezones.
* Players cannot shoot into or out of safezones.

### Farming

* Fields are marked yellow
* Requires sickle/scythe
* Farm → process (where applicable) → sell

* Mining locations are white
* Requires pickaxe or jackhammer

* Processing requires relevant processing license(s)

### Factories (Civilian-Owned)

* Civilians can buy factories for a large upfront cost.
* Factories can produce certain items cheaper than usual.
* Factories can be left producing while the owner leaves and returns later.

Factory Types (examples):

* **General Factory** — $1,000,000 (tools/items like repairkits, jerrycans, medkits, knife, sickle, pickaxe, jackhammer, smokes, etc.)
* **Vehicle Factory** — $2,000,000 (wide range of civilian ground vehicles)
* **Weapon Factory** — $3,000,000 (wide range of weapons/ammo categories)
* **Aircraft Factory** — $30,000,000 (civilian aircraft/helicopters/airliners)

## Illegal Economy Loops

## Civilians — Gang Drug System

* Only civilians can create/join gangs.
* Gang creation cost: **$500,000**.
* Gang areas are marked with orange exclamation + circle when neutral, or gang colour when owned.
* To take control: neutralise if owned, then capture by raising your gang’s flag at the flagpole.

Gang Areas:

* **Gang Area 1 (North):** Cocaine, LSD
* **Gang Area 2 (North):** Heroin, LSD
* **Gang Area 3 (South):** Heroin, Marijuana

Drug selling:

* Dealers are in populated areas between **Rasman and Bastam** (North).

Passive gang income:

* Gangs do **not** receive passive payments from owning gang areas.

## Insurgents — Drug System (No Gangs)

* Insurgents cannot join or create gangs.
* Insurgents can still harvest drugs normally.
* Insurgents process drugs via a drug dealer at insurgent base:

  * Dealer sells the licenses
  * Dealer allows processing there
* They sell drugs the same way via dealer network.

## Meth (North-Only Loop)

* Meth ingredients are **illegal to possess**.
* Two pharmacies:

  * One near Rasman (North)
  * One near Anar and the southern hospital (South)
* Pharmacies are marked with orange dot and the word “Pharmacy”.

Mixing recipe:

* 1× Iodine
* 2× Red Phosphorous
* 2× Ammonia Hydroxide
* 1× Hydriotic Acid

Processing:

* Mix using mixing equipment (can be done anywhere)
* Mixer can become dirty and must be cleaned
* Process at meth lab to create batches
* **15% chance to explode** when processing
* Bulk processing increases explosion severity (often fatal)

## Organ Harvesting (Illegal)

* Civilians and insurgents can harvest organs from dead bodies using a knife.
* Knife purchased from gas station.
* Harvest yields random organs; each has its own sell price.
* Organs sold at **Garmsar near the PMC base**.
* Illegal activity.

## Paychecks, Taxes, Leadership & Voting

### Paycheck Rules

* OPFOR may receive **no paycheck** if the **PDRT Central Bank** was recently robbed (southern central bank lockout condition).
* Paycheck includes:

  * Base paycheck (by faction)
  * License bonuses
  * Rank bonuses
  * Guarding bonuses (+$2,000)
  * Leadership bonuses (President/Chief/Commander)
  * Tax revenue share (President/Commander)

### Base Paychecks (Defaults)

* Civ: $10,000
* PMC: $10,000
* ESU: $12,000
* Cop: $18,000
* OPFOR: $10,000
* Insurgents: $15,000

### Guarding Bonus

* BLUFOR and OPFOR receive +$2,000 for guarding key locations (within ~50m).
* Guarding often grants +1 rank point **except jail markers** which do not give points.

### Leadership & Taxes

* **President (Civ)**:

  * Receives $5,000 + 40% of North shop taxes (then taxes reset)
  * Controls North tax rates at Government Complex
  * Can change laws at Government Complex
  * Voted in by civilians
* **OPFOR Commander**:

  * Receives $10,000 + 30% of South shop taxes (then taxes reset)
  * Controls South tax rates at OPFOR base
  * Voted in by OPFOR players
* **Police Chief**:

  * Receives +$10,000 bonus
  * Voted in by police players

Voting rule:

* Chief/Commander are voted by their team; if only member, can vote for themselves.

### Police Arrest Income

* Police receive the **bail** paid when arresting a player.
* Bail is **max($10,000,000, 5% of bank)**.

### License Pay Bonuses (Examples)

* VIP license adds **+$50,000** per paycheck for all listed factions.

## Missions & Activities

### Insurgent Bombing Targets

* If no bombing target completed for 5 minutes:

  * Insurgents can receive a bombing target
  * Receive a bomb and have 20 minutes to plant
  * Server is notified of target
  * Server is notified when bomb is planted
  * Must defend bomb for 5 minutes
* Rewards:

  * Planter: $300,000 + 30 points
  * Helpers nearby at detonation: $100,000 + 10 points

### Defense Missions (BLUFOR/OPFOR)

* Defend an assigned location for ~10 minutes
* Reward: $200,000 + 15 points

### President Appearance Missions (Civ)

* President appears in a town for ~10 minutes
* Server announces where they will be
* Reward: $200,000
* No points (civs have no rank)

### Patrol Missions (BLUFOR/OPFOR)

* Travel to a location
* Reward: 1 point + money based on travel distance

### Daily Challenges (All factions; via app)

* Random tasks from a mission list (harvest tasks, defend tasks, convoy protection, etc.)
* Requires Daily Challenges app purchase

## Robberies

### Bank Robbery

* Alerts entire server
* Requires a shaped charge (from black market or VIP sources)

### Casino Robbery

* Alerts server
* Casino vault can be robbed when it has accumulated winnings

### Assassination Missions (South)

* Requires 10 players online
* Assigned a player to kill
