# Civilian Guide — Player Manual

## Overview
Civilian play offers a broad range of lawful and unlawful activities. This guide provides clear, practical guidance for new players: how to progress economically, which tools and licenses are required, where to go, and what to watch out for.

## Getting Started
- Starting balance: you begin with funds in the server bank; use ATMs to deposit cash — money kept in the bank is protected on death.
- Licenses: Basic licenses are available at the civilian licensing shop near the civilian spawn. Additional or specialised licenses and weapon licences are available at dedicated authorisation stores (for example, a weapons licensing store south of Rasman and a full license store near the airfields which sells passports, pilot and professional licences).
- Arms Dealer access: Civs may purchase a Western Arms Dealer License ($10,000,000) to access premium military-grade weapons, attachments, equipment, and specialised gear from the Western Arms Dealer (located in the north).

## Mining and Refining (Legal)

- Mining locations: Each ore type has its own mining locations across the map; look for mining area icons in the map legend.
- Tools: Acquire a Pickaxe or, optionally, a Jackhammer (Jackhammers require an engineering qualification) from general supply shops.
- Licence requirement: A Mining License must be purchased at the Civilian Licensing Shop before you can process ore.
- Refining: Bring raw ore to a Resource Refinery to convert it into refined ingots; refined materials sell for significantly higher prices.

Practical notes

- Vehicle choice: Use larger transport vehicles for mining runs to increase efficiency; installing mining equipment on a vehicle automates gathering but requires a large investment.
- Upgrades: Storage expansion options at vehicle mod shops increase your haul capacity and are recommended for mining or large-scale runs.
- Security: Mining in popular areas exposes you to robbery or kidnapping, as these zones attract criminals seeking targets.

## Legal Crop Harvesting

- Legal crops: Apples, tobacco, cotton, oranges, and wheat are harvested at dedicated farm locations marked by yellow circles across the map.
- Tools: A sickle or hoe (purchased from the Farmers Market) is required to harvest.
- Sale and profit: Crops are sold back to the Farmers Market; profits are lower than illegal drug processing but the activity is legal and safe.

## Harvesting and Processing Controlled Substances (Illegal)

- Harvesting: Use harvesting tools (a sickle or hoe) to collect raw materials at drug fields. Each drug type has dedicated harvest locations.
- Licences: Purchase the appropriate processing training from licensing stores. There are basic civilian licensing outlets as well as specialist license sellers for higher-tier certifications.
- Processing: Process raw materials at an authorised processing facility or a dealer-run laboratory; civilians often use gang-controlled labs when available.
- Selling: Processed drugs are sold to designated in-game dealers only. Dealers are located in town centres and other populated areas; drugs cannot be sold through a generic black market interface.

Practical notes

- Gang operations: Only civilians can form or join civilian gangs. Forming a gang requires investment and offers access to gang areas which can be used to stage processing and protection.
- Security: Use larger vehicles and storage upgrades when transporting processed goods. Consider measures to secure transport and extraction routes.

## Meth Production (High Risk)

- Precursors: Precursor chemicals are obtained from pharmacies located in major towns.
- Processing: Processing takes place at the designated meth laboratory in the far north-west of the map (near Nur and Nagara).
- **Risk**: Meth processing may explode when processing at the lab. Plan security and extraction carefully due to the high risk and legal consequences.

## Organ Harvesting (Illegal)

- Tools and sale: A knife purchased from general stores is required to harvest organs from bodies. Organs are sold to specialised buyers in certain towns (for example a buyer near the PMC area).
- Risk: Organ harvesting is highly illegal and will generate significant law enforcement attention if detected.

## Vehicle Modifications

- Licences: Basic driving and truck licences are available at the Civilian Licensing Shop near civilian spawn. Advanced professional licences (pilot, engineer, truck certification) are available at the Full License Store near the airfields.
- Common upgrades: Storage expansion tiers, plate removal, and lock changes help protect valuable cargo. Mining equipment and other specialised installs are available for dedicated operations.

## Garage Storage and Impounding

- All factions can garage vehicles at their spawn to store them persistently between sessions.
- Garage fees: 10% of the vehicle's purchase price.
- Impounded vehicles: An additional $10,000 flat fee applies on top of the normal garage fee.
- Impound locations: Impound lots are located near the police base on the north airfield, near the OPFOR base on the south airfield, and at the location south of Rasman.
- Impounded vehicles remain at the impound lot until retrieved; tow truck drivers can help transport them.

## Bus and Tow Services

- Civilians can become bus drivers or tow truck drivers at the Bus and Tow Depot, located at the impound lot south of Rasman.
- Bus drivers: operate scheduled routes and earn income.
- Tow truck drivers: respond to police impounding requests and transport vehicles to impound lots.

## Vehicle Chop Shops

- Chop shops allow civilians (and insurgents) to sell vehicles they may not own for a reduced payout (illegal activity).
- Locations: Nur (north), and south of the border near Falar and Anar.

## Factories and Long-Term Investment

- Factories can be purchased to manufacture items and vehicles at a reduced cost. They are a long-term investment that can yield steady returns once established.

## Bank Insurance

- Bank money is safe and never lost on death; only cash in your inventory is lost on death.
- During bank raids, a small percentage of your bank balance may be stolen unless you have bank insurance.
- Exception: VIP players automatically protect their bank balance without needing insurance.

## Private Storage

- All factions have private storage at their spawn location; for civilians this is marked as "DMV - Private Storage" at civilian spawn.
- Private storage holds virtual inventory items (ores, crops, drugs, repair kits, refuel kits, medikits, lockpicks, bank insuranceps, etc.); guns and ammunition cannot be stored (they are Arma 3 items).

## Protection and Contracting

- If a PMC is actively working on the server, use the **Contracting App** on your phone to request protection for mining, drug runs, or other risky activities. Contact the PMC to discuss contract terms and rates.

## General Player Tips

- Refer to the **General Player Tips** guide for universally applicable information: night vision, repair kits, medikits, energy drinks, steroids, earplugs, vehicle ammo, speed upgrades, nitro kits, gas masks, and gas station resources.

## Selling and Market Locations

- Legal markets handle refined ores and lawful goods.
- Dealers in populated towns purchase processed illegal goods.

## Common Pitfalls

- Undertaking high-value illegal runs without backup or protection.
- Leaving large sums of cash outside the bank.
- Using undersized vehicles when conducting bulk hauls.

## Typical Progression Checklist

1. Purchase basic harvesting tools and a driver licence. Ensure you have bank insurance.
2. Complete a few mining or legal crop harvesting runs to establish capital.
3. Buy a Mining License and begin refining for larger returns, or continue with legal crops.
4. Invest in vehicle storage upgrades and night vision before scaling up operations.
5. If moving into processing drugs: form or join a gang, secure a processing location, acquire processing training, and plan security for sales and transport. Consider using the Contracting App to hire PMC protection.
